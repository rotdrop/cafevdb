Detailed Description

Collect problem reports and forward them to configured targets (e.g.

submit an issue to Gitlab or Github, or just send an email to a configured email address).

Why? Unfortunately one cannot assume the everybody has a configured standard email client, so the previous idea of just presenting an email link in the frontend does not work. Instead, present a button in the frontend to the end-user which just automagically submits a problem report to configurable locations.
